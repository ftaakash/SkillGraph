Differentiation Strategy: How to Avoid Collision

To succeed, SkillGraph must not just be another "Job Recommender." It must be a "Career Intelligence Platform." Here is the roadmap to uniqueness.

Hyper-Localization

Most papers use global datasets. Focus specifically on Indian Tier-2/3 colleges and local industrial hubs (e.g., Pune Auto cluster, Bangalore IT) to provide relevant data.

Hybrid AI Model

Don't rely on just one algorithm. Combine Knowledge Graphs (for skill relationships) with Collaborative Filtering (successful alumni paths).

Real-Time Pulse

Static databases age fast. Implement a Live Scraper for generic job portals (Naukri/LinkedIn) to flag "Trending Skills" (e.g., 'Prompt Engineering' vs just 'AI').

Outcome Mapping

Existing tools stop at recommendation. SkillGraph should track learning progress and map it to hypothetical salary increases, gamifying the upskilling process.
