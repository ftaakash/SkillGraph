# Research & project artefacts

Material produced while developing SkillGraph as a research project — papers, reports,
design documents, early single-file prototypes and screenshots. None of it is used by
the product at runtime; the application lives in [`../skillgraph-web`](../skillgraph-web).

| Folder | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| `./` | IEEE paper drafts, literature review, project report, CareerOps / V2 plans |
| `docs/` | Architecture & functional documents, test cases, sprint retrospective, IEEE template |
| `prototypes/` | Early standalone prototypes (`index.html`, `skillgraph.tsx`, …) |
| `assets/` | Screenshots and recordings from earlier iterations |

Architecture decisions for the product are recorded in [`../docs/adr`](../docs/adr).
